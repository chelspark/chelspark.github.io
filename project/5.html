Car Spotting App

Car Spotter is a Flutter-based mobile application that enables car enthusiasts to discover, document, and share unique car sightings in real-time. It features seamless navigation between car feeds, detailed post views, and user profiles. The application incorporates location services for geotagging posts and supports full CRUD operations for user-generated content including photos, car details, and profile information.

Key Features

  • Implements both email/password and Google Sign-In authentication with Firebase, creating a seamless onboarding experience
  • Features a chronological feed of car sightings with live updates from Cloud Firestore, displaying user-generated content with rich media support
  • Integrates camera functionality and geolocation services to capture, tag, and upload car sightings with precise location data
  • Offers comprehensive profile customization including image uploads, bio updates, and a personalized grid view of user posts
